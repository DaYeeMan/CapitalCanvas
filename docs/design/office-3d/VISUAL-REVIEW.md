## Constraints to preserve in every comparison

- One shared 20×20 square shell with equal world-space width/depth, aligned northeast wall and no right exit hallway. The old 22×18 rectangle is superseded.
- Shared thicker walls/taller front edges; existing grey base retained without an additional band. Geometry changes preserve room/window alignment and fit/travel bounds.
- V5C target: tall exterior wall/cap tops align with existing glass rooms; low front walls remain 0.58m. Exterior windows have thick cream frames, while glass-room partitions/doors keep dark/tan treatment.
- Home smaller north office repositions toward the reference rear corner; account for surrounding shelves/art, cabinet accessories, planters and lounge. Existing interior positions/inventory can change to achieve the reference layout.
- Home room flush far left; outer pods above lower middle pod; rugs separated; central three-chair round seating without a bookshelf.
- Home Ithaca moves slightly screen-right with coordinated walking clearance; new northeast bookshelf/low counter remain distinct from existing pieces and the bookcase-free central nook.
- Additional cream couch/table/rug sits between Home's two glass rooms along the northwest wall; existing rear-right sofa lounge remains.
- Home far-left meeting room has one tan door at the former glass-door position; its former left tan doorway becomes ordinary glazing. Other room doors stay unchanged.
- Resources glass meeting room right; main northwest glazing stops before the rear office, which has one separate broad window; foreground bench/reading nook retained; deleted storage enclosure stays absent.
- Home inter-room northwest wall has two roughly equal large windows with a narrow cream section and no internal dividers; separate private-office glazing remains, in the same thick cream style.
- Exactly three marked clusters per floor: Ithaca left, Troy center, Delphi right. Secondary furniture remains unmarked.
- Native header/labels, fixed isometric angle, inert activation/About, clear walking routes and motion preferences. Bounded camera movement and header-only floor slides supersede prior locked-camera/wheel-navigation rules.
- Home/Resources/About typography follows the deployed crop while retaining office colors; white cube/prism background stacks follow the office concepts.
- All three nav labels change color on hover; markers/labels remain constant screen size and track zoom/pan. Minimum zoom has zero lasting pan offset but allows resisted drag feedback.
- V6 surroundings are real 3D near/far stacks and receiving shadows on blocks/base. They share the office camera and gently shift with floor progress, with asymmetric breathing space/cropped foreground; no ambient animation or decorative parallax. Keep environment/base out of office fit/pan bounds and clear of the office throughout travel.
